Win Prediction

Complete picks for both teams to see prediction.

How it's computed: every hero pair contributes one observation. Pairs with real data use their observed winrate; pairs with no data are imputed at 50% with a uniform prior (variance 1/12) — so sparse data pulls the prediction toward 50/50. The 95% CI propagates each pair's sampling uncertainty through the win ratio. The (k/N) badge shows how many pairs had real data out of N possible.

Global Ban Rules

In Global Ban & Pick, a hero picked by a team in any game of the series cannot be picked again by that same team in later games.

Bans in the current game still apply normally. Heroes from previous games are shown with a red GLOBAL tag if they are unavailable to the active team.

Draft Order

Phase 1: 2 bans per team (Blue, Red, Blue, Red)
Phase 1 picks: 3 picks per team (Blue, Red, Blue, Red, Blue, Red)
Phase 2: 2 bans per team (Red, Blue, Red, Blue)
Phase 2 picks: 2 picks per team (Red, Blue, Red, Blue)
